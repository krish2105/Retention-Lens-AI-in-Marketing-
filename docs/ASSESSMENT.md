## High-mark strategy
Your outline grades discipline-specific depth, justified decisions, implementation or design experimentation, and reporting. Build explicit before/after evidence rather than a broad feature checklist. Use artifacts from genuine runs and real research; no invented accuracy, customer conversion, or interview quotes.

## Assessment pitfalls
- Scope mismatch: verify professor's current assessment sheet before treating the uploaded accreditation outline as the sole authority.
- Team wording in outline: user is solo. Obtain confirmation whether a nominally group-assessed activity may be submitted individually.
- Submission dates in outlines are academic week markers and may differ from published LMS deadlines.
- The project is separate from all four other MAIB units, even if sharing reusable coding conventions.
