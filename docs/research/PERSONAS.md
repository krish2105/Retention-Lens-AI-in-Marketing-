# Personas and jobs to be done: HYPOTHESES until interviews say otherwise

Status: **HYPOTHESIS.** Written from the course brief and the UCI dataset, not from customers. Each line is
marked with the evidence that would confirm or reject it. Update from `research/interviews/` (R1).

## P1 · Owner-manager of a small wholesaler (200–1,000 trade accounts)

- **Job:** "Keep my best trade customers ordering without hiring another salesperson."
- **Today (assumed):** notices a lapse when a regular stops appearing in the monthly sales report, weeks late.
- **Would pay for:** an early, short list with reasons, and a ready message.
- **Evidence needed:** 3+ interviewees describe noticing lapses late; Van Westendorp range overlaps £30–£80/month.

## P2 · Sales or account manager (1,000–5,000 accounts)

- **Job:** "Spend my calls on accounts I can still save, not ones already gone."
- **Today (assumed):** works from a CRM call list sorted by revenue, not by risk.
- **Needs:** a weekly queue, evidence they can say out loud to a customer, and proof the calls work.
- **Evidence needed:** interviewees say who they call is decided by habit or revenue; interest in a holdout test.

## Opportunity tree (hypothesis)

- Outcome: fewer lost trade accounts, measured by reorder rate vs a holdout.
  - Opportunity: lapses are noticed late → weekly risk queue (built).
  - Opportunity: unsure why an account is slipping → plain-language reasons (built).
  - Opportunity: no time to write check-ins → approved AI drafts (built).
  - Opportunity: no proof outreach works → random holdout and uplift report (built, needs a pilot).
  - Opportunity: data stuck in accounting tools → CSV/XLSX upload (built); direct Xero/Sage connectors (not built).

## Ideal customer profile (hypothesis)

UK or UAE wholesaler or B2B distributor, 200–5,000 active trade accounts, repeat ordering on a cycle of
2–12 weeks, invoice export available, one person who owns customer follow-up.
